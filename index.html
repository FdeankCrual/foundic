Verified Expert Process

We don't classify someone as a Verified Expert based only on their resume.

1
Professional Background Check

We verify their professional history, previous organisations, roles, experience, education, certifications, achievements and relevant professional claims.

2
Professional Journey Meeting

A detailed conversation about their professional journey, the businesses they have worked with, problems they have solved, processes they followed, decisions they made and results they achieved.

3
Real Business Discussion

Where relevant, we discuss actual business requirements and prospects from our pipeline and understand how they would approach and execute those situations.

4
Evaluation

We evaluate their experience, capability, problem-solving, business understanding, execution ability and relevance.

5
Verification

Only then do they become a Foundic Verified Expert.
